# NZME Creative Platform — Elite Bathroomware radio

**Source:** NZME / CreateMe "Strategic Creative Recommendation", September 2026
(deck: *Elite Bathroomware and CODE Interiors Creative Concepts*).
**Owner:** Josh Martinac. Update when NZME refreshes the platform.

---

## 1 · The platform

**"Bathrooms made simple."** Every spot proves it — explains, in the simplest and most
credible way, what it means to customers *and to competitors' customers*.

## 2 · The four pillars (reasons to believe)

Every spot leans on **one** pillar. Rotate them across flights.

| Pillar | NZME line | What it means on air | Proof points we can say |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01 Range** | Endless possibilities | You can have virtually anything you want | 670 sqm showroom · six zones: Vanity, Bath, Tapware, Shower, Toilet, Accessories |
| **02 Quality** | An eye for design | Classic to contemporary, plus in-house design service | In-house design service · named products/finishes |
| **03 Value** | Great value is a given | Clear value without constant discounting. Middle NZ expects value. | No repeated discounts · once-a-year sale (Spring) |
| **04 Expertise** | An expert team, not just sales assistants | Others present and show; Elite guides and advises | ~25 years · family run since day one · "walk out with a finished bathroom, not just a quote" |

## 3 · Sound (the sonic brand)

- **Elite's own piece of music** — "Elite instrumental", up and under the voice.
- **Sung jingle (vocal up):** "Speak to Elite. **We** make bathrooms simple."
  ⚠ The deck mixes "We" and "They" across scripts. A jingle is one recording — confirm
  **"We"** with NZME (matches Concept Two and first-person brand voice).
- **Voices:** two-hander V1 / V2 (Concept One); single female VO (Concept Two).
- **SFX:** real, recognisable sounds that open the spot (birdsong, kids + splashing,
  pen on paper, lawnmower/spring).

## 4 · The two creative routes (the frames the engine writes into)

### Concept One — "Hear that?"  (brand / pillar / seasonal)
```
SFX:     <a sound that IS the benefit>
V1:      Hear that? That's the sound of <the benefit / the moment>.
MUSIC:   Elite instrumental up and under
V2:      <one pillar, proven with one Elite fact>
V1:      <the invitation — what to do>
JINGLE:  Speak to Elite. We make bathrooms simple.
V2:      Elite Bathroomware. The big blue store, 59A Allens Road, East Tāmaki.
```
NZME executions: 1 sanctuary (Quality/Range) · 2 family bathtime (Expertise/family run) ·
3 team taking notes (Expertise) · 4 Spring Sale (Value — **sale phase only**).

### Concept Two — "You have questions. Elite has answers."  (zone / category)
```
VO:      Welcome to Elite Bathroomware and The <Zone> Zone made simple.
VO:      Question 1… 2… 3… And 4…   (the real questions the team asks)
VO:      It's the questions the Elite team asks that make all the difference.
         Speak to an Elite Bathroomware expert today and make it simple.
JINGLE:  Speak to Elite. We make bathrooms simple.
VO:      The big blue store, 59A Allens Road, East Tāmaki.
```
NZME wrote: **Vanity, Bath, Shower.** Not yet written: **Tapware, Toilet, Accessories**
(the other three showroom zones) and **Mirrors / Heated Towel Rails** (Demand Radar
categories — fold into Accessories or Vanity zone unless NZME agrees a new zone).

**This is the demand-driven route.** Demand Radar category → Zone script.

## 5 · Mandatories (every spot)

- **Elite Bathroomware** · **The big blue store** · **59A Allens Road, East Tāmaki**
  (TAH-mah-kee; the deck drops the macron — correct it in every script sent back).
- Jingle as the last-but-one element, address last.
- URL optional (not in NZME's frame). Hours only if read — check holidays in the flight.

## 6 · Out of this platform

The same deck holds a **separate Code Interiors campaign** ("Everything goes together like
it's made to go together", stockist CTA to codeinteriors.co.nz). Different brand,
different platform. **Never mix Code into Elite spots** or vice versa.
